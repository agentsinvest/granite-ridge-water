Turn drip stations 4 and 6 on the Entrance controller back on. Leave the old lawn stations 1 to 3 off, since the entry lawn is now artificial turf.

Meter 3 read zero in every reported hour from September 15 to October 1, including the nights the drip should have run. Trees and shrubs without water in late-summer heat can die, and a mature tree costs far more to replace than the water it uses.
