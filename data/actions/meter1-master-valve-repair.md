In February 2026 Eco Verde found a leak at the master valve on the east side of the park (MV2, repair 1). The master valve is always under pressure, so a leak there runs through the meter even when nothing is watering. That is the most likely cause of the drip between cycles.

Ask whether this repair was ever made. If not, have it repaired, then watch the overnight reads.

One thing to confirm first: the park controller's alerts put lawn stations 8 and 9 on mainline 2 (master valve MV2) and stations 1, 2, 4, and 14 on mainline 1. Stations 8 and 9 are thought to be on meter 2. If that is right, MV2 is on meter 2's line, and a leak there would show on meter 2, not meter 1. Ask the landscaper which meter feeds MV2. On March 30, 2026 the controller saw 58 to 62 gallons a minute on mainline 2 with no station on, for about 20 minutes in the late morning (1,164 gallons): possibly someone working on MV2 that day.
