The current contract asks the landscaper to "monitor and adjust" watering schedules for the season and to check the sprinklers "regularly". It does not say how often, does not ask for a written report, and never mentions gallons, the City bill, or leaks. Every repair is billed on top of the monthly price, and the landscaper pays nothing for water that is wasted.

Things the HOA could ask to add:

* A walk-through of every zone with the water on, at a set frequency (for example monthly), with a short written report of what was found and fixed.
* Reporting any leak, stuck valve, or broken head to Trestle within a set time, such as 2 business days.
* A printout of every controller's settings at each seasonal change, so the HOA knows what is programmed.
* Checking the Waterfluence alerts (or forwarding them to the landscaper) and responding to a high-use alert within a set time.
* A written price list for common irrigation repairs, so repair invoices can be checked.

The contract also says the landscaper is not liable for any damages and the HOA protects the landscaper from claims. Repairs of damage the landscaper causes are free, but water lost before a problem is found is not covered. The board may want its attorney or Trestle to look at that clause before any change.
