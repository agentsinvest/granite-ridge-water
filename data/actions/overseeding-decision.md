The HOA is deciding whether to overseed the park lawn with winter rye this year, overseed less, or skip it.

The savings calculator shows that watering the rye less on its own does not lower the bill, because of how the City sets the cheaper price block. Pairing it with a summer cut on the park lawn does save money.
