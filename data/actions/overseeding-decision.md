The HOA is deciding whether to overseed the park lawn with winter rye this year, overseed less, or skip it.

The savings calculator shows that watering the rye less on its own does not lower the bill, because of how the City sets the cheaper price block. Pairing it with a summer cut on the park lawn does save money.

Skipping the overseed also saves the overseeding charge itself. The landscape contract bills winter rye seeding on top of the monthly price, and the HOA paid $4,137.00 for it in each of 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025. That money is separate from the water bill and is not in the savings figure above. Without rye, the Bermuda lawn goes brown and dormant for the winter; the contract still covers mowing dormant lawn as needed.
