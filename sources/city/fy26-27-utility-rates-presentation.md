# City of Mesa FY 26/27 Utility Rates Recommendation

Water pages that matter for the HOA (landscape accounts):

* Page 11: Service charge, all customers: budget forecast +4.5%, staff recommended +4.0%, AF&E recommended +3.5%. Drought commodity charge: $0.08 now, $0.13 recommended (staff and AF&E).
* Page 12: Usage charge, Non-residential Landscape: +15.0% (budget forecast and recommended). Excess surcharge, Landscape: Tier 1 (over 100% to 150% of winter water average) +15.0%; Tier 2 (over 150% of winter water average) +25.0%, new.
* Pages 13 and 14: Commercial landscape example. Existing excess surcharge $2.99 per thousand gallons above 100% of the winter water average; proposed $3.44 (Tier 1) and $3.74 (Tier 2).
* Page 19: Typical Commercial - Landscape customer (33 kgal a month): $235.91 now; $266.31 staff recommended (+12.9%); $266.04 AF&E recommended (+12.8%).
* Page 61: October 5, 2026 Council action on notice of intent; December 1, 2026 introduction of ordinances; December 8, 2026 Council action on rates; February 1, 2027 effective date.

The page 19 example reproduces with the HOA's own prices: $55.01 service (1 1/2 inch meter) + 30 thousand gallons above the 3 included x $5.95 + 30 x $0.08 drought = $235.91, and $56.94 + 30 x $6.84 + 30 x $0.13 = $266.04 (AF&E). Taxes, the superfund charge, and the environmental fee are not in the example.
