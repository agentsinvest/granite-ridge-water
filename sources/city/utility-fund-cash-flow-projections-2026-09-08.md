# City of Mesa Utility Fund Cash Flow Projections (as of 9/8/2026)

This is a cash-flow projection, not a rate book. For water it gives the planned percent increase in rates by fiscal year (City fiscal years run July to June). It does not list dollar prices, the peak surcharge, service charges, or the drought charge.

The HOA's accounts are Commercial Landscape, which this document does not break out (the FY 26/27 rate presentation gives landscape +15% where general commercial is +13%; sources/city/fy26-27-utility-rates-presentation.md). For reference: the FY 25/26 "WATER Commercial (usage)" increase of 20.00% equals the change in the HOA's usage price from $4.76 to $5.71 per thousand gallons on bills for read periods ending February 2026. Not used to price anything.
