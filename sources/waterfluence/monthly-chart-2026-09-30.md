# Waterfluence monthly chart

What the screenshot shows, transcribed from the image. Chart values were read by eye and are not used as data; exact numbers come from the exports.

* Title: "Monthly 1000 Gallons". View toggles: Ft3, Gallons (selected), $ Cost, Depth.
* Series: "Water Use" (blue line) and "Budget range +/-15%" (green band). The chart covers all 4 meters combined.
* Visible range: October 2023 to September 2026. The range selector underneath shows history back to 2021.

## What it confirms

* The usage unit in the ENERGY STAR export is thousand gallons. The chart's July 2024 point (about 1,100) matches the sum of the 4 meters for the 2024-06-14 to 2024-07-15 read period (1,117), and its November 2024 peak (about 1,250) matches the 2024-10-12 to 2024-11-12 period (1,249).
* Waterfluence keeps a water budget per month. Its budget range is +/-15%, the same disagreement threshold the spec uses.
* Waterfluence can show cost ($ Cost toggle) and has data back to 2021, neither of which has been exported yet.

## What it shows at a glance (read by eye, approximate)

* Use was inside or below the budget band for most months.
* Clear exceptions: a spike well above budget around November 2024 (the post-overseed period), use above budget from about October to December 2025, and a dip below budget around October 2024.
