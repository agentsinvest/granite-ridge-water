## Pages 2 to 4: Landscape Maintenance Contract Specifications

### 1. General clean-up / maintenance schedule

a) Maintained landscape areas will be checked weekly for loose trash & litter.
b) Trash receptacles and dog stations will be checked weekly and emptied as needed.
c) All landscape trimmings and trash collected will be disposed of off-site.
d) Maintained landscape areas will be serviced on a 5 week rotation cycle.
e) Check the playground area wood chips weekly and rake / smooth over as needed.

### 2. Lawn areas

a) All lawn areas will be mowed on a weekly basis (excluding rain days, holidays, and winter lawn germination). Dormant lawn areas will be mowed as needed to maintain a groomed appearance.
b) Provide labor to fertilize lawn areas as needed, up to six times per year. Contract includes the cost of fertilizer.
c) Winter lawn installation (over-seeding of rye grass seed) will be performed at an additional cost.

### 3. Trees

a) Trim and maintain mature trees up to a height of 10 feet to provide clearance over roads and sidewalks.
b) Trim or remove lower tree suckers per rotation cycle.
c) Monitor for signs of pests, disease, or fertilization needs.
d) A separate proposal will be provided for all palm tree trimming and skinning.

### 4. Plants

a) Plants will be pruned to maintain proper size in relationship to the growth space and intended function and to maintain lines of sight.
b) All plant material will be trimmed and managed on rotation using proper horticultural practices.
c) Large plants and hedges will be maintained up to a height of 10 feet from the ground.
d) Monitor and report signs of pests, disease, or fertilization needs.

### 5. Planter beds and decomposed granite areas

a) Planter bed areas will be blown and cleaned on rotation to maintain a clean and uniform appearance.
b) Weeds will be controlled with the use of pre and post emergent herbicides.
c) Natural or NAOS areas are not included as part of this contract or factored into the monthly landscape management price. Any desired work within these areas will be at an additional cost.
d) Erosion repairs and granite replenishment will be estimated separately.

### 6. Irrigation systems

a) Monitor and adjust programmed irrigation schedules to ensure watering rates and frequencies are consistent with seasonal requirements.
b) Sprinkler and drip systems will be regularly checked, adjusted, and repaired as necessary. All repairs will be billed at an additional cost.
c) Irrigation repairs will be performed during regular business hours. In the event of an emergency, technicians are on call and available 24/7 at an additional charge.
d) Any irrigation system damages caused by contractor negligence will be covered and repaired at no additional cost.

### 7. Excluded services (If needed, a separate proposal will be provided)

a) Over seeding / cost of perennial rye grass seed.
b) Storm damage clean up or landscape repairs.
c) Clean-up or repair of any damage caused by acts of God or vandalism.
d) Tree work above 10 feet, tree trimming (crown thinning, structural pruning, etc).
e) Palm tree pruning or skinning.
f) Tree staking or guying.
g) Pruning, removal, or replacement of dead or damaged trees, plants, or other landscape material.
h) Citrus tree fruit picking / removal.
i) Fertilizers, soil amendments, pesticides.
j) Turf weed control (selective herbicide spray application for weeds in lawn areas).
k) Spraying or application of pre-emergent herbicides.
l) Olive tree spraying (annual treatment to prevent fruit).
m) Irrigation system repairs. Irrigation controller repairs or replacements.
n) Manual watering or manual operation of irrigation systems.
o) Planting of seasonal or annual flowerbeds.
p) Erosion repair.
q) Enhancement work or granite replenishment.
r) Non-contracted areas: Landscape clean-up work, weed control, or any other service requests for additional work to non contracted areas of property.
s) Power washing picnic areas or sport court areas.

### 8. Included services (Additional / special requests per RFP)

a) Fertilizer for lawn areas is included in monthly price. Up to 6 applications per year.
b) Lawn aeration, usually performed in May to help with transition to Bermuda.
c) Pre-emergent herbicide application for granite areas.
d) Small doggie bag re-fills will be added and invoiced per box or header.

### 9. Terms and conditions

a) Cancellation: This contract agreement shall continue on a month to month basis until cancelled. Either party may cancel this agreement at any time and without cause by providing thirty (30) days written notice. Upon cancellation notice client agrees to pay all amounts owed to date for services performed.
b) The terms and conditions of this contract are binding to the property and shall remain in force if the property sells or transfers ownership or management.
c) Invoices will be mailed out at the beginning of each month for that months services and payment will be due by the end of that service month, net 30 days from billing. A late charge of 1.5% per month shall be charged on all amounts past due.
d) SLC reserves the right to immediately suspend services or terminate this agreement if payments are past due. In such event SLC shall have no responsibility whatsoever for any consequences thereof.
e) This contract is a service based contract. SLC reserves the right to adjust service days, crew size, assigned personnel, or hours on-site based on seasonal needs or requirements.
f) SLC agrees to carry the necessary licenses and insurance policies and to name the property as additional insured. Copies will be submitted upon request.
g) SLC shall not be held liable for any damages, whether ordinary, incidental or consequential. Client agrees to indemnify, defend, and protect Schnepf Landscape Co from all lawsuits and any costs pertaining to contracted landscaping services and existing condition of the property.

## Review notes (what this means for the water model)

1. **Schedule changes are in the monthly price.** Section 6a makes "monitor and adjust programmed irrigation schedules ... consistent with seasonal requirements" part of the base service. Seasonal adjust, run time cuts, rain pauses, and turning the entrance drip back on are covered. This settles the "assumes the landscaper's contract covers it (to confirm)" note on the schedule options and actions.
2. **Every irrigation repair is extra, and so is any controller work.** Sections 6b and 7m. No labor rate or price list is in the contract, so repair costs still need a quote. The new controller B install is outside the contract.
3. **The landscaper has no water-use duty.** The contract never mentions gallons, the water budget, the City bill, leak reporting, or a check frequency ("regularly checked" in 6b is undefined). The landscaper is paid for repairs and pays nothing for wasted water. This is the gap behind the meter-4 valve running for about 62 hours unnoticed.
4. **Overseeding is a separate charge.** Sections 2c and 7a; $4,137.00 a year on the P&L 2022 to 2025. Skipping it saves that money outright, on top of any water change. Section 8b confirms the lawn is Bermuda overseeded with perennial rye (aeration "to help with transition to Bermuda").
5. **Manual watering is extra (7n).** The park controller logged 6 hours of manual watering on 2025-03-24 (`events.md`). Whether the HOA was billed for it is unknown.
6. **Contradictions in the document.** Fertilizer is included (2b, 8a) and excluded (7i). Pre-emergent herbicide is included (5b, 8c) and excluded (7k). Section 8 is labeled as RFP special requests, so it probably overrides 7, but the contract does not say so.
7. **Liability runs one way.** 9g: the contractor is not liable for any damages, and the HOA indemnifies the contractor. 6d covers repair of damage from contractor negligence, but not the water lost before it is found.
8. **The HOA has leverage.** 9a: month to month, either side can cancel on 30 days written notice. Adding irrigation duties (written monthly check, leak reporting within a set time, controller settings printout each season) can be asked for at any time.
9. **Not water related, noted only:** NAOS areas are excluded (5c); tree work above 10 feet and palms are extra; 1.5% a month late charge (9c).
